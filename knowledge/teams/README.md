# Teams

Context on internal teams — both your own team and the teams you depend on or that depend on you. This is the "who does what and how do they think" layer of the org.

## Suggested files

- `escrow.md` — Your team: mission, success metrics, customers, scope, values, dependencies
- `platform.md`, `orchestration.md`, etc. — Teams you partner with frequently; what they own, who runs them, how they like to work
- `<team>.md` — One file per team is the default; split into a folder (`teams/escrow/scope.md`, etc.) only if a single file gets unwieldy

## Tips

- Update after re-orgs, leadership changes, or scope shifts
- Capture the *unwritten* parts — operating norms, decision styles, current pain points — not just what's in the org chart
- For your own team, this is the doc you'd hand a new PM joining to ramp on team identity
