# Research

User interviews, industry reports, and anything you reference across projects. This is your shared evidence base — insights here inform multiple projects rather than living siloed in one.

## Suggested structure

- `user-interviews/` — Interview notes and synthesis
- `surveys/` — Survey results and analysis
- `market-reports/` — Industry reports and trend analysis
- `insights.md` — Synthesized research insights and recurring themes

## Tips
- Store raw notes here, not in project folders — research often applies to more than one project
- Link from project folders back to research files when a finding drives a decision
- Keep `insights.md` as a living doc of patterns you've seen across studies
